I'm a 4th-year Computer Science student at York University, applying to graduate school for Fall 2027 with the goal of pursuing a PhD. I'm open to both MSc and direct-entry PhD pathways, and I split my time between coursework, research, and outreach to potential supervisors this cycle.

My research is in trustworthy AI: whether large language models’ expressions of uncertainty — both what they say and what their internals encode — stay calibrated when data shifts for causal reasons versus spurious ones. Most calibration work treats distribution shift as one category; I think the causal/spurious distinction changes what “well-calibrated” should even mean.
