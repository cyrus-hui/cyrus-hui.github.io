Independent Researcher — Model Collapse in Self-Trained LLMs

May 2026 – Present

I repeatedly fine-tune GPT-2 Medium and SmolLM2-1.7B on their own outputs (15 seeds, up to 13 generations), with decision rules fixed before reading any results. Distributional metrics signal collapse from the first generation, but standard benchmarks register it late or not at all, and at 1.7B several rise slightly as the model degrades. A calibration gap (log-perplexity minus predictive entropy, from the same forward pass as perplexity) rises monotonically in all 15 seeds.

Since submission, all pre-registered and not yet peer-reviewed: a 63-cell negative control keeps the gap far below its threshold, and in a 126-run severity grid at 345M one fixed threshold separates exposed runs from controls in every evaluable reading. A filtering intervention built on the gap did no better than random removal and is reported as falsified, one of four of my own hypotheses that failed their pre-registered tests. I’m now pre-registering a test of whether the gap after one generation forecasts later damage.

Research Assistant — LLM Health-Impact Behavioral Audit

York University · Advisor: Prof. Enas AlTarawneh · May – Aug 2026

Designed a three-layer behavioral audit of five open-source LLMs on pollution-aware routing advice, grounded in Glasgow road-network and air-quality sensor data (23,700+ responses).
